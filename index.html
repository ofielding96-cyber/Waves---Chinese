Transverse Wave
横波
héng bō
▼
A wave where the oscillations (vibrations) are perpendicular (at 90°) to the direction of energy transfer. Examples: light, all EM waves, ripples on water.
振动方向与能量传播方向垂直（成90°）的波。例子：光、所有电磁波、水面波纹。
★ Think: rope wave — shake up/down, energy travels forward
🔊
Longitudinal Wave
纵波
zòng bō
▼
A wave where the oscillations are parallel to the direction of energy transfer. Creates areas of compression (squashed) and rarefaction (stretched). Example: sound waves in air.
振动方向与能量传播方向平行的波。产生压缩区（密集）和稀疏区（稀疏）。例子：空气中的声波。
★ Think: slinky spring pushed back and forth — compressions travel along it
📏
Amplitude
振幅
zhèn fú
▼
The maximum displacement of a point on a wave from its undisturbed (rest) position. A larger amplitude means the wave carries more energy. Measured in metres (m).
波上某点从其未受扰动（静止）位置的最大位移。振幅越大，波携带的能量越多。单位为米（m）。
★ Louder sound = bigger amplitude · Brighter light = bigger amplitude
↔️
Wavelength
波长
bō cháng
▼
The distance from a point on one wave to the equivalent point on the next wave (e.g. crest to crest, or trough to trough). Symbol: λ (lambda). Measured in metres (m).
从一个波上某点到相邻波上对应点的距离（如波峰到波峰，或波谷到波谷）。符号：λ（拉姆达）。单位为米（m）。
★ Symbol: λ (Greek letter lambda 希腊字母)
🔁
Frequency
频率
pín lǜ
▼
The number of complete waves passing a point per second. Measured in Hertz (Hz). A higher frequency means more waves per second — and a shorter wavelength.
每秒通过某点的完整波的数量。单位为赫兹（Hz）。频率越高，每秒的波数越多——波长越短。
★ 1 Hz = 1 wave per second 每秒1个波 · Higher f = shorter λ
⏱️
Period
周期
zhōu qī
▼
The time taken for one complete wave to pass a point. Symbol: T. Measured in seconds (s). Period and frequency are inversely related: T = 1/f.
一个完整的波通过某点所需的时间。符号：T。单位为秒（s）。周期与频率成反比：T = 1/f。
★ Higher frequency = shorter period · T = 1/f is on the equation sheet
↗️
Wave Speed
波速
bō sù
▼
The speed at which energy is transferred through a medium by the wave. All EM waves travel at 3 × 10⁸ m/s in a vacuum. Calculated using: v = f × λ
波通过介质传递能量的速度。所有电磁波在真空中的速度为3×10⁸ m/s。计算公式：v = f × λ
★ Speed of light in vacuum: 300,000,000 m/s = 3 × 10⁸ m/s
🪞
Reflection
反射
fǎn shè
▼
When a wave bounces back at a boundary between two materials. The angle of incidence = angle of reflection (measured from the normal). Waves can also be absorbed or transmitted at a boundary.
波在两种材料的交界处反弹。入射角等于反射角（从法线量起）。波也可以在边界处被吸收或透射。
★ Angle of incidence = angle of reflection 入射角 = 反射角
💡
Electromagnetic Waves
电磁波
diàn cí bō
▼
Transverse waves that transfer energy and can travel through a vacuum. They all travel at 3 × 10⁸ m/s in a vacuum. They form a continuous spectrum of 7 groups ordered by wavelength/frequency.
横波，能传递能量，可在真空中传播。在真空中均以3×10⁸ m/s的速度传播。它们构成连续的电磁波谱，按波长/频率分为7组。
★ All EM waves travel at the same speed in a vacuum 在真空中速度相同
🌊
Compression & Rarefaction
压缩与稀疏
yā suō yǔ xī shū
▼
Features of longitudinal waves. A compression is a region where particles are pushed together (high pressure). A rarefaction is a region where particles are spread apart (low pressure).
纵波的特征。压缩区是粒子被压缩在一起的区域（高压）。稀疏区是粒子分散开来的区域（低压）。
★ Sound in air: alternating compressions and rarefactions 交替出现的压缩和稀疏

Equations
公式 — AQA Foundation: two equations for Waves · 两个波的公式
📋 Foundation Tier Equations: MUST RECALL = memorise it. GIVEN = provided on the equation sheet in the exam.
必须记忆的公式 vs 考试时提供的公式。
Wave Equation 波动方程 MUST RECALL
计算波速——这是你必须记住的公式 · AQA 4.6.1.2
v = f × λ
v = wave speed 波速 (m/s)
f = frequency 频率 (Hz)
λ = wavelength 波长 (m)
WORKED EXAMPLE 解题示例
A radio wave has frequency 98 MHz and wavelength 3.06 m. Calculate wave speed.
v = f × λ = 98 × 10⁶ × 3.06 = 3 × 10⁸ m/s ✓
一个无线电波频率为98 MHz，波长为3.06 m。计算波速。
v = f × λ = 98 × 10⁶ × 3.06 = 3 × 10⁸ m/s
Period–Frequency Equation 周期–频率方程 GIVEN
周期与频率的关系——考试时会提供，但要理解它 · AQA 4.6.1.2
T = 1 / f
T = period 周期 (s, seconds 秒)
f = frequency 频率 (Hz)
WORKED EXAMPLE 解题示例
A wave has a frequency of 50 Hz. Calculate the period.
T = 1/f = 1/50 = 0.02 s
一个波的频率为50 Hz。计算周期。
T = 1/f = 1/50 = 0.02 s
